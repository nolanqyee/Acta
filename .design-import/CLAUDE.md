# Acta project rules

Design system of record: `Acta Design System.dc.html`. Section **09 · House rules** is the
canonical list of standing decisions; every flag raised in review gets added there.

## Banned outright
- **No status pill with a coloured dot inside it.** State status as plain mono text, or a
  filled stamp carrying real words.
- **No em dashes** anywhere in product or marketing copy, comments or data. Use a colon,
  comma or full stop. En dashes are fine in date ranges (2023–2027).
- **No decorative colour.** Accent and secondary carry meaning only (see below). No
  accent/white/secondary rotations on glyphs, separators or badges.
- **No hand-drawn third-party logos.** Use a drop slot and wait for the real asset.
- **No selection aura / glow on graph nodes.** Selection reads by size and fill.

## Always
- Neubrutalism: 3px ink rule, hard offset shadow (8px panels, 3px controls and rows), no
  blur, no scrims. Rounded: 18px panels, 12px rows, 999px pills.
- **Every button carries the 3px offset shadow**, primary through tertiary.
- One ink (#10120f) with alphas 72 / 52 / 18 / 8. Three surfaces: paper #f4f1e9,
  panel #f9f7f1, card #ffffff.
- Brand: primary #6fb3e8 (settled, actionable), secondary #ff2861 (unresolved, needs the
  user's judgement). Both are props, and ink is picked by real contrast.
- Type: Charis SIL **700** for anything named, Figtree for prose and buttons, Space Mono
  10px / .16em caps for labels. Ladder: 72 (marketing) / 48 / 30 / 20 / 15 / 13 / 11 / 10.
- Spacing ladder: 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24, plus 56 page margins.
- Graph: straight edges, circular nodes, one detail panel left and one right-hand panel at
  a time, camera nudges instead of relayout.
- Endeavor kinds differ by **structure** (diploma, spec sheet, registrar card, service
  record, liner note, placard, ticket stub, standing tally), never by hue.

## Planned, not built
